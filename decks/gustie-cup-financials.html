Key Assumptions & Risk Mitigations
Risk Mitigation
AI API costs spike Model-agnostic architecture; can swap Claude / GPT / open-source. Costs declining 20-30%/yr.
Slow district sales cycles Free pilot → grade outcome evidence → paid conversion. Algorythm partnership provides early distribution.
LMS platform changes LTI 1.3 is an open standard — not vendor-locked to Canvas or Moodle.
Competitor copies approach 2-year head start, live classroom data moat, self-improving strategies.
AI regulation in schools Full transparency dashboard. Teacher always in control. Designed for compliance.
