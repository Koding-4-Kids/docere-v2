The Problem

A problem every school faces.
Nobody has solved it.

AI MISUSE

Students copy, don't learn

Students paste homework into ChatGPT, get the answer, and move on. Zero understanding gained. Teachers can't stop it.

ZERO VISIBILITY

Teachers are in the dark

No teacher in America can see how their students use AI tools. It's a complete black box in every classroom.

STATIC TUTORS

AI tutors never improve

Khanmigo, ChatGPT, Cognii — none measure whether students actually learned. Same tutor today as a year ago.

The Solution

Docere: an AI tutor that gets better at teaching.

Plugs into existing LMS

Canvas & Moodle via LTI 1.3. Auto-syncs courses, assignments, grades, rosters. Teacher clicks one button.

Teaches, doesn't tell

Socratic questioning grounded in actual course materials. Guides students to answers — never gives them directly.

Measures & improves

Every interaction scored on 4 dimensions. Scores linked to real grades. Teaching strategies evolve weekly.

One-click setup. Teachers add Docere to their LMS. Everything syncs automatically. No configuration, no training, no IT tickets.
