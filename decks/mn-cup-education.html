Our Approach

LMS-native. Zero-setup. Self-improving.

LMS-Native Integration

LTI 1.3 standard. Works with Canvas & Moodle (95% of US K-12). Auto-syncs courses, assignments, grades, rosters, PDFs. Teacher clicks one button.

CanvasMoodleLTI 1.3OAuth 2.0

Pedagogical Scoring

Every interaction scored on 4 dimensions: helpfulness, clarity, understanding gain, engagement. Hybrid scoring: 70% LLM-as-judge + 30% rule-based heuristics.

Process VerificationHybrid Scoring

Strategy Evolution

Teaching strategies modeled as a multi-armed bandit (UCB1). Weekly cycle: mutate top performers, prune underperformers. Scores linked to real LMS grades via 14-day attribution window.

Teacher Experience

More visibility. Zero additional work.

At-Risk Alerts

Automatic detection of struggling students with severity levels and recommended interventions. Teacher is notified before students fall behind.

Concept Heatmap

Class-wide view of which concepts generate the most confusion. Helps teachers prioritize instruction and identify curriculum gaps.

Conversation Logs

Every AI-student interaction, searchable and filterable. Teachers see exactly what students ask, how the AI responds, and where students struggle.

Student Profiles

Individual cards with engagement level, concept mastery graph, grade trajectory, and narrative summary of learning patterns.

Compliance

Built for district procurement.

🔒

FERPA

No student data leaves the LMS scope. JWT auth. Role-based access. All data flows through authenticated LTI channels.

👶

COPPA Ready

Under-13 protections built in. No direct data collection from minors. Parental consent framework.

🛡

SOC 2 Path

Architecture designed for SOC 2 Type II certification. Audit trail on all data access. Encryption at rest and in transit.

🇧

Data Sovereignty

All data stored in US regions. No training on student data. Students can't override Socratic mode.

Pedagogical guardrails: The AI never gives answers directly. Teachers can see every interaction. Parents can request data deletion. Students cannot bypass Socratic tutoring mode.
