Historical Context

Education was always personal

470 BC
Socrates taught through dialogue. One student, one teacher, one question at a time.
Medieval Era
Apprenticeships. Masters passed knowledge to a handful of students. Every learner was known.
1800s
Industrial Age. We needed to educate millions. So we built factories for learning — same pace, same test, every student.
1900s–2000s
Standardized everything. Textbooks, lectures, bell curves. Efficient — but the personal connection? Gone.

For Context

What is an LMS?

A Learning Management System is the software every school uses to run courses — the operating system for education.

Course Materials

Syllabi, assignments, readings — uploaded and organized by the instructor.

Grades & Submissions

Students submit work, instructors grade it. The gradebook is the system of record.

Rosters & Enrollment

Every student, every section, every role — managed in one place.

The Problem — Students

What students actually need

No personalization

Every student gets the same content at the same pace. Struggling? Too bad.

AI gives answers, not understanding

ChatGPT solves the problem for you. You get the grade but learn nothing.

No memory between sessions

Every time you open a chatbot, it's a blank slate. It forgets what you struggled with.

No connection to your course

Generic AI doesn't know your syllabus, your assignment, or what your professor expects.

The Problem — Teachers

What teachers are dealing with

Zero visibility into AI use

Students use ChatGPT and teachers have no idea what's being asked or copied.

Can't identify struggling students early

By the time a student fails a midterm, it's too late. No early warning system.

Grading takes forever

Hours spent on spreadsheets, manually entering grades, cross-referencing systems.

No tool connects everything

IXL for practice, Kahoot for quizzes, Turnitin for plagiarism. Nothing talks to each other.

How It Works

Zero setup. One click. Done.

Plugs into Canvas or Moodle via LTI — like Turnitin or Zoom. No migrations, no IT ticket.

Auto-syncs content

Pulls syllabi, assignments, materials from the LMS automatically.

Remembers every student

Tracks struggles, learning patterns, and concept mastery over time.

Teaches, doesn't answer

Socratic questioning guides students to understanding.

Teacher dashboard

Full visibility into every student-AI interaction. At-risk alerts.

Gradebook sync

Upload a spreadsheet, auto-maps to the LMS gradebook.

Schedules meetings

Struggling student? Docere books time with the instructor.

Under the Hood

Backed by months of research
on a proprietary algorithm

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing

Per-concept mastery tracking using probabilistic inference — not just quiz scores.

UCB1 Strategy Selection

Multi-armed bandit selects the best teaching strategy for each student state. 27 context buckets.

Self-Improving Loop

Every interaction scored on 4 dimensions. Weekly, top strategies mutate. Weak ones are pruned.

Semantic Memory

Vector embeddings of every interaction. The tutor recalls by meaning, not just recency.
