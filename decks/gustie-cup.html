A 2,000-Year-Old Problem

The best education has always been one-on-one.

🏫

470 BC — Socrates

Teaching through dialogue

One teacher, one student, one question at a time. Learning was a conversation. The gold standard of education.

⚙️

1800s — Industrial Age

We traded personalization for scale

We needed to educate millions. So we put 30 kids in a room with one teacher. Same pace, same content, same test.

💻

1900s–2000s — Standardized

New tools, same model

Textbooks, computers, online platforms. Efficient — but the personal connection Socrates had? Gone.

Every leap in education changed the tools.

But none of them changed the model.

The Digital Shift

Then we digitized the factory.

Schools adopted Learning Management Systems to move courses online. Assignments, grades, materials — all in one place. But the model stayed the same: one-size-fits-all.

The LMS digitized the classroom. It didn't personalize it. Every student still gets the same content at the same pace.

For Context

What is an LMS?

A Learning Management System is the software every school uses to run courses — the operating system for education.

Course Materials

Syllabi, assignments, readings — uploaded and organized by the instructor.

Grades & Submissions

Students submit work, instructors grade it. The gradebook is the system of record.

Rosters & Enrollment

Every student, every section, every role — managed in one place.

The Problem — Students

AI remembers your name.
It still doesn't know your classroom.

No personalization

Every student gets the same content at the same pace. Struggling? Too bad. Bored? Also too bad.

AI gives answers, not understanding

ChatGPT solves the problem for you. You get the grade but learn nothing. The next exam? Lost.

No memory between sessions

Every time you open a chatbot, it's a blank slate. It doesn't remember what you struggled with last week.

No connection to your course

Generic AI doesn't know your syllabus, your assignment, or what your professor expects.

The Problem — Teachers

Millions of students use AI every day.
Their teachers can't see any of it.

84%
of students use AI
26%
of teachers caught it

That means: for every student a teacher catches using AI, there are three more they never see.

What teachers are dealing with

40% of schools have banned AI — while 84% of students use it anyway.

68% of teachers received zero training on AI this year.

50% of teachers say AI made them more distrustful of student work.

It's a complete black box.

What if there was a system that could...

? Remember every conversation with a student — and actually learn from each one?
? Already know your classroom — without anyone having to set anything up?
? Show teachers exactly which students are struggling — before they fall behind?
? Guide students to the answer through questions — instead of just handing it to them?
? Connect directly to grades, assignments, and materials — automatically?
? Prove whether its teaching actually worked — with real data?
