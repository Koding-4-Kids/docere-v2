# Student Study Habits Survey — Google Form Setup

## Form Title
**How Do You Actually Study?**

## Form Description
Quick 2-min survey about how you study and the tools you use. Your answers help us build better tools for students. All responses are anonymous.

---

## Questions

### 1. What year are you?
**Type:** Multiple choice
- Freshman
- Sophomore
- Junior
- Senior
- Grad student

### 2. What's your major area?
**Type:** Multiple choice
- STEM (CS, Engineering, Math, Science)
- Business / Econ
- Humanities / Social Science
- Arts / Design
- Health / Pre-med
- Other

### 3. How do you usually study for exams? (Select all that apply)
**Type:** Checkbox (multi-select)
- Re-read notes / textbook
- Practice problems / past exams
- Flashcards (physical or digital)
- Watch YouTube / video explanations
- Study groups
- AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
- Tutoring (in-person or online)
- I mostly wing it

### 4. What tools do you currently use for studying? (Select all that apply)
**Type:** Checkbox (multi-select)
- ChatGPT / Claude / AI chatbots
- Quizlet
- Anki
- Notion
- Google Docs / Slides
- Khan Academy
- Chegg / Course Hero
- None really — just my notes

### 5. If you use AI for studying, what do you use it for? (Select all that apply)
**Type:** Checkbox (multi-select)
- Explaining concepts I don't understand
- Summarizing readings / notes
- Generating practice questions
- Checking my work / homework help
- Writing assistance
- I don't use AI for studying

### 6. What's your biggest frustration when studying?
**Type:** Short answer (1-2 sentences)

### 7. If an AI tutor could do ONE thing perfectly for you, what would it be?
**Type:** Short answer (1-2 sentences)

### 8. Would you use an AI tutor that's connected to your actual course materials and grades?
**Type:** Multiple choice
- Yeah, that sounds super useful
- Maybe — depends on how it works
- Probably not
- No, I prefer studying on my own
